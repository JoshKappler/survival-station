How to Use Your Survival Station

A simple guide. No experience needed. You cannot break it by clicking.

💡 The most important rule

This computer works with no internet. For food, plants, or medical choices, never trust just one source — and never trust the AI assistant alone. Always check the written guides too. When you are not sure, do not do it.

1. Turning the computer on

  1. Press the power button on the computer once. Wait about a minute.
  2. If it asks, sign in the same way you always do.
  3. You do not need internet, Wi-Fi, or a phone line. Everything is already inside this computer.

After you sign in, the Survival Station may open all by itself. If it does, you can skip to step 3.

2. Starting the Survival Station

Look at the desktop (the main screen with the picture background) for this button:

🏕 Survival Station — START HERE
  1. Double-click it (click it twice, quickly).
  2. A calm “Starting your Survival Station…” screen appears. This is normal. Wait a few seconds.
  3. The big main screen opens by itself. You’re ready.

It is safe to click START HERE more than once. It will not start things twice or break anything.

4. Asking the Assistant a question

  1. On the main screen, click 🗣️ Ask the Survival Assistant.
  2. Click in the box at the bottom and type your question, for example:
    “How do I treat a deep cut?” or “How do I make water safe to drink?”
  3. Press the Enter key (or click the send button).
  4. Wait a few seconds. On this computer the answer comes a few words at a time — that is normal. Be patient.

Tips: Use simple, clear questions. You can ask follow-up questions in the same box. If an answer stops early, just type “please continue”.

5. Showing the Assistant a photo (plant or wound)

If the Assistant has a photo or paperclip button, you can show it a picture — for example a plant you found or an injury.

  1. Click the photo / paperclip button in the Assistant.
  2. Choose the picture from the computer.
  3. Type what you want to know, e.g. “What plant is this? Is it safe?”

⚠️ Very important about photos

The AI is only making its best guess, and it can be wrong. A wrong guess about a plant or a wound can be dangerous. Always double-check the answer in the Food & Foraging guide, the Medical guide, or the Encyclopedia before you act. If you cannot confirm it, treat the plant as not safe and do not eat it.

6. Looking things up in the Encyclopedia

  1. On the main screen, click 📖 Encyclopedia.
  2. Type a word in the search box (for example water purification) and press Enter.
  3. Click a result to read it. Click the ← back arrow to return.

This is a full copy of Wikipedia and other books, stored inside the computer. No internet needed.

7. Opening the Maps

  1. On the main screen, click 🗺️ Maps.
  2. Drag with the mouse to move around. Use the + and − buttons (or scroll wheel) to zoom in and out.

8. The safety rules

  • Never trust one source alone. Check the guides and the Encyclopedia.
  • Never trust the AI alone for food, plants, or medical choices. It can be wrong.
  • When unsure, don’t. If you cannot confirm something is safe, do not eat it, drink it, or do it.
  • For serious wounds or illness, follow the Medical & First Aid guide carefully.

9. If something seems stuck

If a screen freezes, a button does nothing, or the Assistant will not answer:

  1. Restart the computer: click the Start button (bottom-left), then the power icon, then Restart.
  2. After it starts up again, double-click 🏕 Survival Station — START HERE.
  3. Give it a minute. Everything should work again.

Closing the window does not harm anything. You can always reopen it from START HERE.

10. Power & the screen going dark

To save power, the screen turns off after about 10 minutes of no use. The computer keeps working — it does not go to sleep, so the Assistant and Encyclopedia stay ready.

  • To wake the screen: move the mouse or press any key (like the Shift key). Your screen comes right back.
  • When you are finished for a long time: you may shut down or hibernate from the Start menu to save battery. Next time, turn it on and click START HERE.
  • Keep it plugged in when you can, so it is always ready in an emergency.

🎉 That’s everything

When in doubt: click START HERE, ask the Assistant, and check the guides. Stay calm, take your time, and remember — when unsure, don’t.

==============================================================
  MEDICAL - READ THIS FIRST - SAFETY WARNING
==============================================================

These guides are NOT a substitute for trained medical care.
They are here to help you do your best when no doctor, nurse,
or hospital is available.

IN AN EMERGENCY, DO THE BASICS FIRST - in this order:

  1. STOP THE BLEEDING.
     Press hard and directly on the wound with a clean cloth.
     Keep pressing. Add more cloth on top; do not lift to peek.
     For a limb that won't stop bleeding, a tight tourniquet
     above the wound can save a life - note the time.

  2. KEEP THE AIRWAY OPEN.
     Make sure nothing blocks the mouth/throat. If unconscious
     and breathing, lay them on their side (recovery position)
     so they don't choke. If not breathing, begin CPR.

  3. PREVENT SHOCK.
     Lay the person down, raise the legs a little (unless that
     hurts an injury), and keep calm. Shock can kill even when
     the visible injury seems minor.

  4. KEEP THEM WARM.
     Cover with blankets/clothing. Cold makes bleeding, shock,
     and pain worse.

THEN: stay calm, look up the specific problem in these books,
and act steadily. Reassure the patient - fear makes things worse.

Watch the person closely and keep checking: Are they awake?
Breathing? Bleeding stopped? Warm? Getting better or worse?

Also see the First Aid manuals in the manuals folder

Stay calm. Use these books. Do the basics. You can do this.

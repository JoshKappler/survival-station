==============================================================
  FOOD & FORAGING - READ THIS FIRST - SAFETY WARNING
==============================================================

NEVER eat a wild plant or mushroom based on one source, a single
picture, or the AI alone. MISIDENTIFICATION CAN KILL YOU.

Many deadly plants and mushrooms look almost exactly like safe,
edible ones. Some poisons act slowly - you may feel fine for a
day, then suffer fatal organ damage. There is no general "test"
that makes an unknown mushroom safe.

RULES:
  1. Cross-check EVERY wild food against MULTIPLE guides in this
     library before eating it. If the guides disagree, DO NOT eat it.
  2. When you are not 100% certain, DO NOT eat it. Hunger for a
     day will not kill you; the wrong plant can.
  3. Be extra careful with: mushrooms, white/yellow berries,
     plants with milky sap, umbrella-shaped flower clusters
     (parsley/carrot family - includes deadly hemlock), and
     anything bitter or soapy-tasting.
  4. Use the UNIVERSAL EDIBILITY TEST (described in the US Army
     Survival Manual, FM 21-76, in the manuals folder) ONLY as a
     last resort for plants, never for mushrooms or fungi.
  5. Cook wild foods when in doubt; some are only safe cooked.
     Introduce any new food in a SMALL amount first.
  6. Keep children and the vulnerable away from untested foods.

WHEN UNSURE, DON'T. A confident guess is the most dangerous thing
in the woods. Live to forage another day.

(Guides in this folder: USDA Home Canning, Common Weeds of the
United States, Common Edible Mushrooms, TM 10-420 Emergency Food
& Poisonous Plants. Also see FM 21-76 in the manuals folder.)

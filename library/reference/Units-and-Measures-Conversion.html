Water & Disinfection (practical)

Boiling: bring water to a rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above 6,500 ft / 2,000 m) to make it safe to drink.

Bleach (unscented, 5–6% sodium hypochlorite): add 2 drops per liter/quart (8 drops per gallon) of clear water; double for cloudy water. Stir, wait 30 minutes; a slight chlorine smell should remain. (Per CDC/EPA guidance.)

1 gallon of water ≈ 8.34 lb (3.78 kg). Plan ~1 gallon (4 L) per person per day for drinking + basic hygiene.
